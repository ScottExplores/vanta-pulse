# Visual fidelity ledger

Reference: `concept-gameplay.png`

Release implementation: `implementation-gameplay.png`

Production source: `https://vanta-pulse.vercel.app/` (captured July 31, 2026)

| Visual target | Release result | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eclipsed neon-city atmosphere | The playable stage preserves the central eclipse, deep black negative space, luminous skyline, and nocturnal sci-fi mood. | Pass |
| Cyan/magenta geometry language | Rails, progress, skyline edges, shell, collectibles, and hazards retain the concept's cyan/magenta signal split with white used only for focus. | Pass |
| Sparse HUD hierarchy | Progress remains a single top rail; score and flow stay left; pause/audio stay isolated at top right; the input hint remains low and centered. | Pass |
| Immediate gameplay legibility | The runner, floor, collectibles, and hazards use distinct silhouettes and high-contrast edge light at gameplay scale. | Pass |
| Depth, motion, and glow | Parallax skyline layers, reflection grid, particles, bloom, chromatic separation, scan texture, and glitch bursts reproduce the concept's depth without flattening the playfield. | Pass |

The concept intentionally shows a late-run spectacle. The shipped opening is calmer and more spacious so hazards read instantly, then increases visual density with speed, flow, events, and later campaigns. Photosafe and reduced-motion settings further suppress nonessential effects while preserving the art direction.
